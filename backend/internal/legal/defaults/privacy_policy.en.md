## 1. Who we are
Phsar ({{site}}) is operated by {{company}}, {{address}}. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it. Contact us at {{email}} or Telegram {{telegram}}.

## 2. Data we collect
- **Account:** name, phone number, Telegram or Facebook account ID and name (we receive only your public profile — not your password, friends or posts), preferred language.
- **Orders:** delivery recipient, phone for the courier, address, map pin location, order items, payment status. We never receive your bank card or bank login — payments are made in your banking app.
- **Sellers:** store details, ID document, tax number, pickup points, bank account for payouts (stored encrypted).
- **Photos you upload:** for example photos for a return request or a photo search. Photo-search pictures are used only to find products and are not kept.
- **Technical:** IP address, device and browser type, and security logs (sign-ins, admin actions).

## 3. How we use it
To create your account, process payments, deliver orders, handle returns and support, pay sellers, prevent fraud, meet legal duties, and improve Phsar. We send service messages by Telegram or SMS. We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. Who we share it with
- The **seller** of your order and the **courier** receive the name, phone and address needed for delivery.
- Service providers: ABA Bank / PayWay (payments), Telegram, Facebook (sign-in), PlasGate (SMS codes), Google Maps (address search), Anthropic (automatic translation of product text — no buyer data), and our hosting provider.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it
Order and payment records are kept as long as Cambodian tax and accounting rules require. Seller ID documents are kept while the store is active and for the period the law requires afterwards. Security logs are kept for up to 180 days.

## 6. Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. See [Data deletion]({{base}}/legal/data-deletion). Some records (for example paid orders) must be kept for legal reasons even after your account is deleted.

## 7. Security
Data is sent over HTTPS. Sensitive data (bank accounts, authenticator secrets, service keys) is encrypted. Staff access is limited by role, protected by two-step sign-in and recorded in an audit log.

## 8. Cookies
We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to remember your cart and language. We do not use advertising cookies.

## 9. Changes
We will post changes on this page with a new version date.
