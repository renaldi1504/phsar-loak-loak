## 1. Selling on Phsar
This agreement applies to every store that sells on Phsar ({{site}}), operated by {{company}}. By opening a store you confirm that you have the right to sell your products in Cambodia and that the information you give us is true.

## 2. Verification (KYC)
- We verify your identity (ID card or passport), your seller type and, where applicable, your tax registration (TIN) and Ministry of Commerce licence.
- Individual sellers without a TIN may sell up to the turnover limit set by Phsar.
- Every store needs at least one verified pickup point where our courier collects parcels.
- ID documents are stored encrypted and viewed only by authorised Phsar staff; each view is recorded.

## 3. Products
- List only products you have in stock, with honest descriptions, real photos and a short video.
- Products are checked automatically when you send them. Most go live straight away; some (new stores, restricted categories, unusual prices) wait for a manual review. Phsar may review, hide or remove any product at any time, before or after it appears. Used items must show their condition clearly.
- You set the return window and warranty for each product; they are shown to buyers and you must honour them.
- Prohibited: counterfeit goods, weapons, drugs, alcohol and tobacco sold without a licence, adult content, live animals, and anything illegal in Cambodia.

## 4. Orders
- Accept or decline each paid order within 2 hours. Pack it and mark it ready for pickup at your pickup point.
- Repeated late responses, cancellations or misleading listings can lead to warnings, hidden products or store suspension.

## 5. Money
- Buyers pay Phsar. After delivery, the amount is held for the hold period set by Phsar (currently a few days) for returns and complaints, then added to your balance.
- Phsar charges a commission on each order (currently 8% of the item price unless another rate is shown in your Seller Center). Phsar pays the payment-gateway fees.
- Payouts are transferred to the bank account you registered, on the schedule set by Phsar. A changed bank account is held for 48 hours for security.
- If a return is approved after you were paid, the refunded amount (minus Phsar's commission share) is deducted from your balance.

## 6. Returns and disputes
You must answer return requests within 48 hours. If you reject a request or do not answer, Phsar decides. See the [Return & Refund Policy]({{base}}/legal/returns).

## 7. Content and translation
You give Phsar permission to show, translate and promote your product content on Phsar and its social channels. You are responsible for having the rights to your photos and videos.

## 8. Ending the agreement
You can close your store at any time after open orders and returns are finished. Phsar may suspend or close a store that breaks this agreement or the law.
