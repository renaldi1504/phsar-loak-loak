## 3. Prices and payment
- Prices are in US dollars (USD); the riel amount is shown for reference only.
- You pay the full amount to Phsar with KHQR (ABA Mobile or any Cambodian bank app). Phsar does not offer cash on delivery.
- One checkout can contain items from several stores; it becomes one order per store.
- A payment QR code is valid for a limited time (shown on the payment page). If it is not paid in time, the order is cancelled and the items are released.

## 4. Orders and delivery
- After you pay, the seller has 2 hours to accept the order. If the seller declines or does not answer, the order is cancelled and you are refunded in full.
- Delivery is currently available in Sihanoukville only and is free. A Phsar courier collects the parcel from the seller and brings it to the address and map pin you gave. Please keep your phone on so the courier can reach you.
- If delivery fails because you could not be reached, we will contact you to arrange another time.
