## 1. When you can return
- **Change of mind:** within the return window shown on the product page (for example "Returns: 7 days"), counted from delivery. The item must be unused, complete and in its original packaging. Products with "No returns" cannot be returned for change of mind.
- **Wrong, damaged or not as described:** report within the product's return window or within 3 days of delivery, whichever is longer — even for "No returns" products.
- Not returnable: items damaged after delivery by the buyer, used personal-care items with a broken seal, and items missing parts you received.

## 2. How to request a return
1. Open **My orders**, choose the order and tap **Request a return**.
2. Choose a reason, describe the problem and add photos (strongly recommended for damaged or wrong items).
3. The seller has **48 hours** to accept or reject.
4. If the seller rejects or does not answer, **Phsar reviews the case** (usually within 2 working days) and decides: full refund, partial refund, or rejection.

## 3. Sending the item back
If the item must be returned, a Phsar courier collects it from you free of charge in Sihanoukville. Keep the item and packaging until the case is closed.

## 4. Refunds
- Approved refunds are paid back to the bank account that paid the KHQR, usually within **7 working days** after approval.
- Partial refunds may be agreed, for example for a small defect you want to keep.
- If an order is cancelled before delivery (seller declined, did not answer, or out of stock), you are refunded in full automatically.

## 5. Disputes
Phsar's decision closes the case on the platform. You may still contact us at {{email}} or Telegram {{telegram}}, and your rights under the Law on Consumer Protection of Cambodia are not affected.
