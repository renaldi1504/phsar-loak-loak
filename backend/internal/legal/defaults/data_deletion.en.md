## Delete your Phsar data
You can ask us to delete your Phsar account and personal data at any time.

**How to ask**
1. Send a message to Telegram {{telegram}} or email {{email}} with the subject "Delete my data".
2. Tell us the phone number, Telegram username or Facebook name you used to sign in. If you checked out as a guest, include an order number.
3. We may ask you to confirm the request from the same Telegram or phone number, so that nobody else can delete your account.

**What happens next**
- We delete or anonymise your account, saved addresses and sign-in links (Telegram, Facebook, phone) within **30 days**.
- Records we must keep by law — for example paid orders, invoices and refunds — are kept for the required period with your name and contact details removed where possible.
- If you have an open order or return, we finish it first.

**Signed in with Facebook?**
You can also remove Phsar in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → Phsar → Remove. Then send us the request above so we also delete the data stored at Phsar.

**Sellers** must first close their store (no open orders, returns or unpaid balance). Store tax records are kept as the law requires.
